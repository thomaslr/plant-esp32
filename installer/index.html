🌿 Plant Monitor ESP32-C3

Official 1-Click In-Browser Web Flasher. Installs bootloader, Dual-OTA partition table, and application firmware in one shot.

  1. Connect your ESP32-C3 SuperMini via a USB-C data cable.
  2. Put board in download mode (Hold BOOT → tap RST → release BOOT).
  3. Click the button below and choose your ESP32-C3 serial port.
Your browser does not support Web Serial. Please use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
