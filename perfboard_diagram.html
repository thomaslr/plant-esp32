Permanent through-hole soldered assembly for ESP32-C3 SuperMini with FQP30N06L MOSFET & dual star-ground buses.

📖 Assembly Guide (MD) 🔌 Breadboard Diagram
⚠️ PERFBOARD HOLES ARE ISOLATED: Unlike a breadboard, there are NO internal copper traces. Every connection must be made via a solder bridge, bare bus wire, bent lead, or hookup wire.
Top = Row R • Bottom = Row A
100%

Board Architecture 18 × 24 DIP

• Row Orientation: R (Top Edge) down to A (Bottom Edge).
• Microcontroller: ESP32-C3 SuperMini mounted in dual 1×8 female headers (Cols 3–10, Rows L & F).
• USB-C Access: Extends off Left edge (Cols 1–2).
• Sensor Terminals: TB2 (Soil) & TB1 (Echo) in Row Q, entries face UPWARDS off Row R.
• Pump Terminal: TB3 in Row B, entries face DOWNWARDS off Row A.
• MOSFET: FQP30N06L in Row C (Cols 18–20) with direct 2-pad solder bridge to TB3 Drain!
