Supply Divider Architecture Zero-Crossover

• R1 (10kΩ): Sits vertically between N6 (+5V) and K6 (Node).
• R2 (10kΩ): Sits horizontally along Row K between K6 (Node) and K4 (GND).
• C1 (100nF): Spans between L4 (GND) and K6 (Node). Solder bridged K6 $\to$ J6 (Node) and L4 $\to$ K4 $\to$ J4 (GND).
• Bridge K6 $\to$ J6: Connects 2.5V node directly to ESP32 GPIO 0.
• Bridge K4 $\to$ J4: Connects divider/filter ground to ESP32 GND (Pin J4).
