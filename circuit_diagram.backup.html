Plant Monitor — Quarter-Size Breadboard (Cols 1–18 + Power Rails)

Dedicated Dual Rails (+5V, +3.3V, Common GND) • Screw Terminals in Row A • Pure Direct Wiring

Power Rail Configuration

Your breadboard's dual power rails simplify the entire wiring:

  • Top (+) Red Rail: +5V (USB VBUS) — Feeds ESP32 5V pin, TB1 Ultrasonic, and TB3 Pump.
  • Top (-) Blue Rail: Common GND.
  • Bottom (+) Gold Rail: +3.3V System — Fed from ESP32 3V3 pin; powers TB2 Soil Sensor.
  • Bottom (-) Blue Rail: Common GND (connect to Top GND under the board).

Direct Rail Conditioning

  • 10k/10k Divider: Resistor 1 plugs directly from Top 5V Rail to Col 5. Resistor 2 plugs from Col 5 directly into Top GND Rail. 100nF cap across Col 5 & GND.
  • Echo Stepper: 1kΩ from Col 12 (Echo) to Col 8. 2kΩ from Col 8 directly into Top GND Rail.
  • Pump Driver: Source & 100k pull-down plug directly into Bottom GND Rail.
  • Bulk Cap (470µF): Plugs directly into Top Rails (+5V & GND) near the pump!
