Passive Components & Functions

Part Value / Location Circuit Function
R1 10 kΩ • Top 5V Rail → Col 4 Voltage Divider Top: Drops the 5V USB rail down to safe 2.5V.
R2 10 kΩ • Col 4 → GND Rail Voltage Divider Bottom: Sets the exact 1/2 division ratio for ADC1.
C1 100 nF • Col 4 → GND Rail ADC Filter Capacitor: Low-pass filter to smooth 5V readings and prevent ADC sampling droop.
R3 1 kΩ • Col 13 → Col 8 Echo Stepper Top: Current limiter and upper divider leg from 5V HC-SR04 Echo.
R4 2 kΩ • Col 8 → GND Rail Echo Stepper Bottom: Steps 5V Echo pulse down to $3.33\,\text{V}$ to protect GPIO 4.
C2 100 nF • Col 12 Row H → Col 14 Row H Soil Sensor RF Filter: Absorbs high-frequency Wi-Fi transmission bursts from distorting soil readings.
R5 1 kΩ • Col 6 → Col 12 Gate Damping Resistor: Suppresses high-frequency parasitic ringing on MOSFET Gate.
R6 100 kΩ • Col 12 → GND Rail Gate Pull-Down Resistor: Holds Gate firmly at 0V during boot/reset so pump stays strictly OFF.
Q1 FQP30N06L • Cols 12, 13, 14 Logic-Level N-MOSFET: Low-side pump motor switch across 3 adjacent holes (Pin 1 G: Col 12, Pin 2 D: Col 13, Pin 3 S: Col 14). Fully saturated at 3.3V logic level ($V_{GS(th)} \le 2.5\text{V}$, $R_{DS(on)} < 0.05\,\Omega$).
D1 1N4001 • Col 13 → Col 15 Flyback Snubber Diode: Clamps reverse inductive voltage spikes from pump motor coils.
C3 470 µF • Top Rails (+ & -) Bulk Decoupling Capacitor: Supplies inrush current surges to prevent ESP32 brownouts.

Terminal Orientation (Unobstructed Access)

  • TB1 (Top Edge • Row J): Faces UPWARDS off top edge. 4-pin Ultrasonic (5V, TRIG, ECHO, GND).
  • TB2 (Top Half • Row F): Faces DOWNWARDS into open Center Trench. 3-pin Soil (Cols 10, 12, 14: VCC via G2, AOUT, GND).
  • TB3 (Bottom Edge • Row A): Faces DOWNWARDS off bottom edge. 2-pin Pump (DRAIN, +5V).
