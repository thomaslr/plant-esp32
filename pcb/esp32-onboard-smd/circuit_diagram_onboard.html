⚡ Board Architecture Highlights

Direct Monolithic ESP32-C3 Integration: Replaces the SuperMini daughterboard with an official ESP32-C3-WROOM-02 module soldered directly to the board, eliminating all socket assembly and loose pins.
• Native USB-C CDC/JTAG: Full-speed hardware USB communication for effortless flashing and serial debugging.
• AMS1117-3.3 Power Rail: Solid 1000 mA LDO with multi-stage MLCC filtering (10µF + 100nF + 22µF) eliminates motor brownouts.
• Dual Dedicated ADCs: Real-time concurrent sampling on ADC1 (GPIO 0 for 5V, GPIO 4 for Battery) with only 21 µA battery drain.
• Anti-Backfeed Protection (D2 SS34): Blocks current from backfeeding into solar boost / battery packs when USB is plugged in.
• Hardware Buttons & LEDs: Onboard tactile RESET & BOOT buttons + green PWR and blue STAT LEDs.
