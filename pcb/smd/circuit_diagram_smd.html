⚡ Rev 2.3 Turnkey Architecture

🎯 Dual Independent ADC Channels (No Jumpers!):
• 5V Supply Rail: Monitored on GPIO 4 (ADC1_CH4) via R1/R2 (100k/100k) + C1.
• 18650 Battery: Monitored on GPIO 0 (ADC1_CH0) via R8/R9 (100k/100k) + C6.
• Ultrasonic ECHO: Digital input on GPIO 20 via R3/R4 (1k/2k).
• Strapping Protection: GPIO 2 left unconnected to guarantee 100% reliable booting.
• Pinout Reference: LastMinuteEngineers Reference
🛡️ Anti-Backfeeding Schottky Diode (D2):
SS34 (3A 40V, SMA package) physically blocks USB-C current from backfeeding into the external battery charger or solar boost module when flashing firmware.
